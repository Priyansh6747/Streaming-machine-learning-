    E01 — Metric v2 correction (Exp-01/02 revision)¶

    Status: concluded (clean three-model panel: DeepRiver, frozen RF, timing oracle) · Artifact: experiments/results/e01/ · Command: uv run python -m experiments.run --exp e01

    1. Why this experiment exists¶

    Exp-01 and Exp-02 reported a 294× mean damage gap and a 24× worst-vs-best gap. Both were inflated by a defect in the metric, not by the models:

    • damage_area integrated from τ to the recovery point.
    • On 8 of 11 seeds the recovery rule collapsed to recovery = τ (the model never left its band), so the integral covered a single sample.
    • Streaming damage was therefore truncated; frozen damage — which never recovered — was integrated over the full window.
    • Both numbers were upper bounds, and the viva brief already flagged the defect as a known weakness.

    E01 re-runs the same pilot with a metric that cannot be gamed this way.

    2. What metric v2 changes¶

    Metric Definition Why
    damage_ref (primary) Σ max(κ_ref − κ(t), 0) over a fixed window from τ κ_ref is shared across the model panel, so a weak model cannot score zero damage by having a low own-baseline
    damage_full Same, from τ to the end of the stream Prices the whole remaining stream, not just the pre-registered window
    AUE mean error minus the timing-oracle error over the fixed window Dimensionless; oracle is an RF refit at τ on pre-drift data
    T_exit / T_recover Recovery only counts after a real band exit Removes the “0 = instant recovery” artefact
    recovery_rate Fraction of seeds with a real recovery Replaces the misleading mean of a floored recovery time

    The fixed window is 5,000 samples for the abrupt SEA stream. The recovery band is 0.95·κ_ref with a 500-sample stretch.

    3. Setup¶
    Stream SEA, one continuous U(0,10)³ draw
    Length 60,000 samples · warm-up 12,000 · drift at τ = 30,000
    Drift abrupt: x₀ + x₁ > 8 → x₀ + x₁ > 9
    Noise 10% label noise
    Seeds 11: 42, 0…9
    Streaming DeepRiver RollingClassifier, RNN 3→16→2, window 500
    Frozen RandomForestClassifier(100), fit once on the warm-up
    Oracle RF refit at τ on the last 12k pre-drift samples (knows when)

    The panel is DeepRiver, frozen RF, and the timing oracle. The earlier artifact that also contained HT/HAT — caused by a host-default bug that has since been fixed — is archived at experiments/results/_archive/e01_5model/; every number below comes from the clean rerun.

    The two panels are deliberately separate: damage_ref is the primary damage metric, AUE is the excess over the timing oracle. AUE is negative for DeepRiver because the timing oracle is weak (its damage_ref is 505, almost identical to frozen), not because DeepRiver is impossible.

    DeepRiver's damage is lower on 11/11 seeds. With the clean panel the worst DeepRiver seed (217.9) sits below the best frozen seed (295.2) — 0.74× its damage. The mean gap is 6.9×, and the full-stream gap is 10.8×.

    5. Verdict¶

    Pre-registered condition Required Measured Verdict
    Frozen never recovers every seed 0/11 PASS
    DeepRiver damage_ref lower ≥10/11 seeds 11/11 PASS
    AUE vs timing oracle ratio or better DeepRiver −0.0472, frozen +0.0010 PASS — mechanism, not upper bound

    The claim survives; the magnitude shrinks. A streaming model that keeps learning pays about 6.9× less damage in the pre-registered window than a frozen model (10.8× over the rest of the stream), and the frozen model never recovers. The old 294× and 24× are retired.

    6. What this retires¶

    • 294× mean damage gap → corrected to 6.9× (damage_ref) or 10.8× (damage_full) on the clean panel.
    • 24× worst-vs-best → retired. The worst DeepRiver seed is 0.74× the best frozen seed; the worst-case claim was a truncation artefact. The earlier four-model panel's 1.27× is superseded by the clean rerun.
    • “835 samples” → seed 42 still recovers in 835 samples under the corrected rule, but recovery is 8/11, with values 285–4,279. The number was seed luck; the distribution is the result.
    • “Damage area and dip depth are the trustworthy metrics” → too generous. damage_ref is the trustworthy one; damage_full is the one that prices the whole stream; recovery time is meaningful only conditional on an exit.

    7. Caveats to keep in the report¶

    1. Panel composition (resolved). The panel is DeepRiver, frozen RF, and the timing oracle. The pre-fix four-model artifact is kept at experiments/results/_archive/e01_5model/ for auditability; the numbers here are the clean rerun and supersede it.
    2. AUE is not an upper bound. The timing oracle knows when the drift happened but is refit only on pre-drift data; its damage is almost identical to frozen. AUE therefore measures “excess over a weak oracle,” not “distance from the best possible model.” Use damage_ref as primary.
    3. One synthetic stream. This is still SEA, one abrupt drift. The detector matrix, real streams, and the Delay Tax are the next gates.
