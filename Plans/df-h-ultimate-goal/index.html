    DF-H: deep hybrid drift detector — ultimate-goal roadmap¶

    Status: approved 2026-11-14 Supersedes: the breadth-first E05–E15 detector roadmap. E01–E17 remain as controls and evidence; the detector track is re-centred on DF-H. Related: docs/Phase0:Research/idea/scope.md §5.3 and §6.5, docs/Reports/Nov14.md, docs/Reports/Results.md, docs/Reports/E17-real-detector-map.md.

    Readable summary: Roadmap v2. Evidence snapshot: Nov 14 post-mid-term report.

    Status update (2026-11-14): E18 and E19 ran and failed their pre-registered gates (best deep AUC 0.616 vs error 0.598; DF-H 3rd overall, 7.09, behind STEPD/SEED). E20 is not run under the pilot-first rule. E21 now carries the headline: the fixed-delay Lemma-1 synthesis shows label-free Stage 1 warning at 70–513 samples vs 2,120–3,269 for the error-only panel at L = 2,000; the full regimes remain scaffolded. E22 adds the focused capability check: DF-H is 3rd overall (7.34), ahead of HDDM-W, ADWIN, HDDM-A and PageHinkley, behind only SEED/STEPD. See E18, E19, E21 and E22.

    0. The decision¶

    0.1 Ultimate goal¶

    Build DF-H, a two-stage drift detector:

    1. Stage 1 — label-free deep monitor. A deep representation model (DriftFormer's encoder, with DeepRiver/an autoencoder as baselines) watches the input stream and emits drift signals without labels: masked-feature SSL loss, embedding shift, head-bank disagreement, predictive entropy.
    2. Stage 2 — supervised confirmation. Classical error-stream detectors (SEED, STEPD, ADWIN, HDDM-A) run on the host's mistakes. A Stage-1 warning is confirmed only when a Stage-2 detector fires inside a short window.
    3. Fusion. A calibrated, pre-registered rule reports both the warning time (Stage 1) and the confirmed detection time (Stage 2).

    0.2 Success criteria¶

    Primary — KDD'26 immediate-label benchmark. Same protocol as the current reproduction: 7 datasets × 4 drift types × 2 transitions × 50 trials, Hoeffding Tree host, same signal/anchor convention.

    Criterion Bar
    Overall average rank top-2 (currently SEED/STEPD at 2.12)
    Weak cells best on at least two of feature_permutation, feature_filtering, recurring
    False alarms FAR no worse than ADWIN
    Per-dataset robustness no dataset rank worse than 4
    Stretch #1 overall, or a clear win on all three weak cells

    Secondary — the Delay Tax benchmark (the novel contribution).

    Criterion Bar
    Fixed delay L DF-H warning time grows by ≤ L + c, while error-only detectors are floor-bound at τ + L
    Partial labels DF-H keeps higher recall than the best error-only detector at p = 0.5 and p = 0.1
    Selective labels DF-H warns earlier on unlabelled drift; confirmation still needs labels
    Lemmas Lemma 1 (floor), Lemma 2 (1/√p), Lemma 3 (batch FAR) verified empirically

    Negative result is acceptable. If DF-H does not beat the baselines, the delay-tax measurement and Lemmas 1–3 still stand — the scope already declares this a publishable negative result. The detector track cannot sink the project.

    0.3 Non-goals¶

    • Do not optimise DriftFormer as a classifier. E14 shows it is weaker than ARF on real streams (κ 0.524 vs 0.724; damage_ref 284.9 vs 198.0). It is a signal extractor, not the benchmark model.
    • Do not replace the KDD Hoeffding Tree host. A deep host would make the comparison a different benchmark.
    • Do not train a deep model per KDD trial. Use a frozen per-dataset encoder and precompute signals.
    • Do not expand the E05/E08–E13 matrix. Those are deferred.
    • Do not build Chapter 2 (the service) before the detector result.

    1. Why this is the right goal¶

    The current evidence points to exactly one missing artefact: a detector that uses information the scalar detectors cannot see.

    • No scalar detector wins everywhere. E04: HDDM-A is best on all four synthetic shapes on the static host, but recurring is only F1 0.500. E17: on real Elec2 the default is host-conditional — HDDM-A wins all four shapes on the static HT, but ADWIN wins abrupt 1.000 vs 0.400 and recurring 0.770 vs 0.593 on the adaptive HAT. The best-host mean is ADWIN 0.935 vs HDDM-A 0.910.
    • The external benchmark has headroom but a low ceiling. KDD'26: SEED/STEPD rank 2.12 (mean F1 0.300/0.303); HDDM-W 3.56; HDDM-A 3.87. Absolute F1 is low, so a better signal matters — but naive combinations raise false alarms (E02: EWMA makes ADWIN 0.548/1k and KSWIN 7.667/1k vs a 0.1 target).
    • Feature-space detection is the strongest arm on feature drift. ABCD(X) is cited as rank 1.0 abrupt / 3.3 gradual on feature permutation. A deep representation monitor is a nonlinear version of that idea.
    • The mechanism already exists in DriftFormer. _step() computes a masked-feature SSL loss and feeds an internal ADWIN; forward() returns the four head logits and the cls_out embedding. The detector is an instrumentation and fusion problem, not a new model.
    • The hybrid is already the declared contribution. scope.md §5.3 names "label-free early warning + delayed supervised confirmation" as the headline; §6.5 argues it is the information-maximal architecture. This plan makes it the centre instead of a late chapter.

    2. What "based on DeepRiver" means, and the scope amendment¶

    DriftFormer/DeepRiver supplies the representation, not the classifier.

    • KDD benchmark host: Hoeffding Tree (same as the current reproduction). The deep monitor is an additional, label-free detector channel.
    • Synthetic/real validation host: Hoeffding Tree and HAT/ARF, to test host-conditional behaviour.
    • Scope amendment (dated note in scope.md §9): deep models are permitted as label-free signal extractors for the hybrid detector. They are not the benchmark classifier and do not replace the classical baseline. This is the implementation of §6.5, not a reversal of the classical scope.
    • Fallback if deep is disallowed: replace Stage 1 with a classical feature-space monitor (PCA/KS/linear MMD, ABCD(X)-style). The fusion, gates, and delay benchmark are unchanged.

    3. Architecture¶

    3.1 Stage 1 — label-free deep signals¶

    All signals are computed per window (default 500 samples, stride 100) from a frozen encoder, and normalised against the first 20% of the stream.

    Signal Definition Why it should help
    ssl_loss Masked-feature cross-entropy from the SSL head on the window The encoder's own reconstruction/feature model becomes surprised
    embedding_shift Standardised L2 distance between window mean/covariance of cls_out and a reference window; optional linear MMD Direct P(X) change; strong on feature permutation/filtering
    head_disagreement Mean pairwise JS/TV across the four head softmaxes Concept uncertainty without labels
    entropy Predictive entropy of the active head Confidence collapse
    internal_adwin Existing ADWIN on the SSL loss Cheapest deep signal; baseline
    feature_ks Max per-feature KS statistic vs the reference (classical control) Shows how much the deep monitor adds over a simple test

    Encoder training. Per dataset, train DriftFormer's encoder with the SSL objective on a fixed un-drifted reference sample (20k rows, or all rows for small datasets). Freeze it. No labels are used for Stage 1. For synthetic streams, train on the first 12k warm-up samples or a separate reference seed.

    3.2 Stage 2 — supervised confirmation¶

    Use a fixed panel, not a test-time best-detector oracle:

    • SEED and STEPD — the KDD'26 top two.
    • ADWIN — best on the adaptive host in E17.
    • HDDM-A — best on the static synthetic host in E04.
    • Raw mistake channel only; E02 rules out EWMA.

    3.3 Fusion¶

    Pre-register the rule; report all variants as ablations:

    Variant Rule
    S1 Stage 1 only (label-free)
    S2 Best fixed scalar detector only
    OR Stage 1 or Stage 2
    AND Stage 1 and Stage 2 within W_confirm = 2,000
    2of3 Stage 1 + SEED + ADWIN, two of three
    2of3_nodeep SEED + ADWIN + HDDM-A, two of three (control)

    v1 uses a fixed weighted z-score with a threshold calibrated to FAR ≤ 0.1/1k on E02 and the pre-drift 20% of each trial. v2 replaces the fixed weights with a small logistic regression / gradient-boosted model trained on window features.

    Leakage rule. Any learned fusion is trained with leave-one-dataset-out on KDD and never tuned on the test trials. Synthetic streams are training-only.

    3.4 Warning vs confirmed alarm¶

    For the delay benchmark, report two times separately:

    • T_warn: first Stage-1 alarm (no labels needed).
    • T_confirm: first Stage-2 confirmation after the label arrives.

    This is the honest version of the hybrid: Stage 1 can beat the reaction floor, Stage 2 cannot. Lemma 4 (pure posterior drift) is exactly the case where Stage 1 has no power and Stage 2 must do the work.

    4. Benchmark strategy¶

    4.1 KDD'26 immediate-label benchmark¶

    • Protocol: unchanged from experiments/kdd_bench.py — 7 datasets, 4 drift types, abrupt/gradual, 50 trials, 100k row cap, shuffled per trial, Hoeffding Tree host.
    • Convention: headline uses --signal correct --gradual-anchor code (reproduces the published F1 tables); the paper-protocol defaults are a robustness check.
    • Deep signal path: precompute in the main environment; kdd_env loads .npz signals and runs pure-NumPy fusion. No torch in kdd_env.
    • Baselines: SEED, STEPD, HDDM-A, HDDM-W, ADWIN, DDM, S1, S2, 2of3_nodeep, and the current best 2of3.
    • Metrics: KDD F1, FAR/1k, NDT, average rank over 56 cells, per-drift-type rank, per-dataset rank, Friedman/Nemenyi, bootstrap CIs over trials.
    • ABCD/ABCD(X): keep cited from the paper; do not run the expensive arm.

    4.2 Delay Tax benchmark (replaces E15)¶

    • Regimes: fixed L ∈ {0, 10, 100, 1000}; geometric mean L; partial p ∈ {1, 0.5, 0.1}; selective (only flagged samples labelled); batch arrival b ∈ {1, 16, 128}.
    • Hosts: Hoeffding Tree and ARF.
    • Detectors: SEED, STEPD, ADWIN, HDDM-A, DF-H.
    • Metrics: T_warn, T_confirm, FAR, recall, downstream damage/recovery.
    • Lemmas: L1 floor (T ≥ τ + L), L2 power loss (1/√p), L3 batch FAR inflation, L4 identifiability (pure posterior drift).
    • E06 stays as the Lemma-1 pilot; E21 is the full benchmark.

    4.3 Controls¶

    • No drift: E02, for FAR calibration.
    • Pure posterior drift: label_swap on unchanged P(X) — Stage 1 must be blind; Stage 2 must catch it.
    • Feature drift: feature_permutation/feature_filtering — Stage 1 should win; Stage 2 may add little.
    • Host swap: HT vs HAT — the E17 host-conditional result must hold.

    5. Phased roadmap and gates¶

    Phase 0 — close the in-flight work (time-boxed)¶

    1. Commit the working tree in logical units; keep the 99 MB ARFF and the JVM crash log out of git.
    2. Finish E03's 11-seed run (20/22 units done); regenerate the summary and schedule report.
    3. Document E14 with acceptance rules; render E17 figures.
    4. Reconcile the E01 74.8/515.0 and E02 FAR numbers across live docs.
    5. Add the new Roadmap v2 page and nav.

    Exit: a clean enough tree to start the detector without mixing unrelated changes into its commits.

    Phase 1 — instrumentation (E18 scaffold)¶

    • DriftFormerLearner.signal_snapshot() returns per-block ssl_loss, head_disagreement, entropy, and cls_out statistics.
    • New DeepSignalMonitor owns the windowing, reference statistics, and normalisation.
    • Artifact writer emits signals.npz beside the existing series.npz.
    • Refactor kdd_bench.py to expose a pure-NumPy build_trial_stream(task, data) used by both the KDD runner and the deep precompute.
    • Tests: shapes, finiteness, determinism, stream parity.

    Exit: a 1,500-sample SEA smoke run and a 1-seed E17 run produce finite, deterministic signal arrays.

    Phase 2 — signal-validation gate (E18)¶

    • Extract signals on SEA, Hyperplane, Elec2, and a KDD pilot: 2 datasets × 4 drift types × 2 transitions × 5 trials = 80 trials.
    • Measure windowed AUC for "drift within the next W samples" on held-out datasets; compare every deep signal against the raw error-rate baseline.
    • Go/no-go gate: at least one deep signal has AUC ≥ 0.75 on held-out data and beats the error-rate AUC by ≥ 0.10. If not, stop or fall back to the classical feature monitor.

    Exit artifact: experiments/results/e18/ + docs/Reports/E18-deep-signals.md.

    Phase 3 — DF-H v1, rule-based fusion (E19)¶

    • Implement the fusion variants; evaluate on synthetic + E17 + the KDD pilot (2 datasets, 5 trials).
    • Gate: top-2 on the pilot; best on at least one feature cell; FAR ≤ ADWIN; no pilot dataset rank worse than 3.
    • Exit: artifact + report; decide whether v2 is worth the full KDD run.

    Phase 4 — DF-H v2, learned fusion + full KDD (E20)¶

    • Train the fusion on synthetic + a subset of KDD datasets; leave-one-dataset-out.
    • Full 7 × 4 × 2 × 50 KDD run with precomputed deep signals.
    • Gate: top-2 overall; best on at least two weak cells; FAR ≤ ADWIN; no dataset rank > 4. Stretch: #1 overall.
    • Exit: final KDD artifact + docs/Reports/E20-deep-kdd.md; Results row.

    Phase 5 — Delay Tax benchmark (E21)¶

    • Add label regimes to the harness: fixed, geometric, partial, selective, batch.
    • Run DF-H vs SEED/STEPD/ADWIN/HDDM-A on the delay regimes.
    • Gate: T_warn ≤ L + c; T_confirm matches the best error-only detector plus the expected label delay; partial/selective recall improvement; Lemma 1–3 slopes measured.
    • Exit: delay artifact + docs/Reports/E21-delay-detector.md; headline contribution.

    Phase 6 — write-up and minimal system¶

    • Restructure the final report around the detector:
    • Measurement lab + baseline detector map (E01/E02/E04/E17/KDD).
    • DF-H architecture + immediate-label benchmark (E18–E20).
    • Delay Tax benchmark + lemmas (E06/E21).
    • Minimal system/demo or a clearly scoped Chapter 2.
    • The mid-term report stays frozen; corrected numbers go into the final report.

    8. Risks and fallbacks¶

    Risk Mitigation / fallback
    Deep signal has no discriminative power Phase-2 gate stops early; fall back to a classical feature monitor (PCA/KS/linear MMD) with the same fusion
    KDD host mismatch Keep the Hoeffding Tree host; deep model is only a signal channel
    Leakage in the learned fusion Leave-one-dataset-out; pre-register the rule; no test-trial tuning
    Deep compute cost Frozen per-dataset encoder; batched GPU inference; cached signals; pilot-first
    DriftFormer is a weak classifier Never use it as the host; use its encoder only; compare against an autoencoder/DeepRiver representation
    False alarms Raw channel only; E02-calibrated threshold; confirmation window; FAR target
    Scope says deep is out Dated scope amendment: deep is a label-free signal extractor, not the benchmark classifier; classical fallback ready
    Delay floor cannot be beaten Report warning vs confirmed separately; Lemma 1 is a theorem, not a failure
    KDD protocol quirks Use the public-code convention for the headline; run the paper convention as a robustness check
    Laptop thermal ceiling Reduced worker counts / taskset; hand heavy runs to the user

    10. Assumptions and open decisions¶

    1. "Beat the benchmark" = top-2 overall + best on at least two weak cells. #1 overall is the stretch goal. If #1 is mandatory, tighten Phase-4 gates and add an ABCD-style feature ensemble, but do not promise it.
    2. Deep is allowed as a label-free signal extractor. The benchmark host stays classical. If the teacher forbids deep, use the classical feature-monitor fallback.
    3. Frozen per-dataset encoder, not per-trial training. This keeps the KDD benchmark feasible and the comparison fair.
    4. Pilot-first compute. No full 50-trial deep signal run until the 80-trial pilot passes.
    5. Cut list is accepted. E05, E08–E13 (except the lite ablations) and the full Chapter 2 are deferred.
    6. Heavy commands are handed to the user. Per the project rule, the agent writes and statically checks the scripts; the user runs the long jobs.

    If any of these is wrong, the affected phase changes; the architecture and gates do not.

    11. Definition of done¶

    • DF-H implemented as a reusable detector adapter with tests.
    • E18 signal report with a clear go/no-go verdict.
    • E19 pilot result; E20 full KDD result with a DF-H row beside SEED/STEPD.
    • E21 delay benchmark with Lemmas 1–3 measured (or a documented negative result).
    • New docs pages wired into mkdocs.yml; Results/progress/scope updated.
    • The mid-term report remains frozen; the final report is written from saved artifacts.
    • One-command reproduction for every result.
